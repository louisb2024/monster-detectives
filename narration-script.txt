THE UNRAVELED MUMMY — Narration Script
Scene-by-scene voiceover, one paragraph per panel/beat. Written for a single narrator voice (not character dialogue).

1. THE CALL
Deep in Monster City, on the spookiest night of the year, Sheriff Lou's voice crackled over the radio. The mummy statue in the town square had been completely unwrapped — and something told Madeline this wasn't going to be an ordinary case.

2. CAUGHT IN THE ACT
The team raced to the square to find bandages scattered everywhere. Before anyone could figure out what happened, Kobe came barreling past with a giant strip of bandage in his mouth — and took off running.

3. THE CHASE
Through pumpkin patches and piles of leaves, Kobe led the whole team on a wild chase across town, howling with joy the entire way.

4. THE TOOLSHED CLUE
Behind an old toolshed, they finally found him — sitting on a fallen scarecrow, tail wagging, with a chewed-through rope lying nearby. Olivia leaned in with her magnifying glass. This rope hadn't just snapped. Something had eaten straight through it.

5. THE REVEAL
Piece by piece, the detectives put it together: the rope, the scarecrow, the trail of bandages leading straight behind the shed. There was only one culprit clumsy enough — and hungry enough — to have done all of this.

6. CASE CLOSED
With the mummy statue rewrapped and the mystery solved, the Monster Detectives had one more thing to catch — Kobe, tearing off after a shadowy figure across the square, howling at the full moon as he went. Another case had already begun.
