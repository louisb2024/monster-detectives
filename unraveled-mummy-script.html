monster detectives — episode 1

The Unraveled Mummy

a 15-minute shooting script

When the town square's mummy statue turns up completely unwrapped, the Monster Detectives chase the only clue they've got — a hyperactive puppy running off with the evidence in his mouth.

MADELINE · 9
The Leader
OLIVIA · 9
The Clue Finder
VEDA · 6
The Goofball
KOBE · 1
The Puppy, No Lines

Production Notes

Voices: three ElevenLabs voices for Madeline / Olivia / Veda. Kobe never speaks — bark, pant, and scuffle sound effects only, so he costs nothing in voice credits.

Static vs. generated: Scenes 1, 4, 5, 6 are dialogue-heavy — build these as still panels with pan/zoom (cheap). Scene 3 (the chase) is the one worth spending real image-to-video credits on — it's the highest-motion, highest-payoff sequence in the episode.

Color key: each character's name is tinted to match their poster color, so the cue color alone tells you who's talking when you're skimming for VO recording.

COLD OPEN — EXT. TOWN SQUARE — NIGHT 0:00–0:45

A full moon over rooftops. Bats cross its face. Jack-o'-lanterns flicker down the street toward a stone building marked MONSTER POLICE.

TITLE CARD: "Somewhere in town, past the last porch light, something is stirring..."

Shot note: single slow-push establishing shot. Good candidate for a short, cheap image-to-video pass — camera motion only, no character animation needed.
SCENE 1 — INT. MONSTER POLICE HQ — NIGHT 0:45–2:00

MADELINE straightens her badge, addressing the room. OLIVIA is already halfway out the door. VEDA sits on the desk with a bag marked "EVIDENCE" that is mostly candy.

MADELINE

Okay, everyone. We need to stay focused tonight.

OLIVIA

I already found footprints on the porch!

MADELINE

Olivia, we haven't even gotten a case yet.

OLIVIA

I know. I just like finding things.

VEDA (mouth full)

I found a candy wrapper! Or maybe I found lunch.

MADELINE

Veda —

VEDA

Just checking.

A radio crackles on the desk.

DISPATCH (V.O.)

Attention, Monster Detectives — the town square mummy statue has been completely unwrapped. Bandages everywhere.

MADELINE

That's our case. Let's roll, team.

They grab their gear. A beat. Everyone looks around.

OLIVIA

Wait. Where's Kobe?

MADELINE

KOBE?!

SCENE 2 — EXT. TOWN SQUARE — CONTINUOUS 2:00–4:00

The team arrives to find the mummy statue completely bare, its bandages unraveled across the square like streamers.

OLIVIA (crouching)

Look — drag marks. Whatever did this went that way.

VEDA

Maybe the mummy got really hot and took its own wrap off.

MADELINE

Everyone knows mummies don't get hot, Veda.

VEDA

You call yourself a detective? Mummies live in the desert — of course they get hot!!

KOBE races INTO frame — a huge strip of mummy bandage trailing from his mouth like a cape.

VEDA (looking at Olivia and Madeline with contempt)

Kobe's already on the case while you two are playing Barbies. I think he found something!

MADELINE

KOBE, DROP IT!

Kobe runs faster. Ears flapping. Tail a blur.

OLIVIA

He definitely found something.

MADELINE

After him!

CUT TO:
SCENE 3 — THE CHASE — EXT. TOWN STREETS — CONTINUOUS 4:00–7:00
Shot note: this is the big spend. Fast-cut chase, mostly visual gags — worth generating as real motion (Runway/Pika/Kling) rather than static panels. Sound design carries it: paws slapping pavement, crashing pumpkins, rustling leaves.

Kobe barrels down the sidewalk, bandage flying behind him. He clips a pumpkin display — it topples — and he tumbles head over paws through a pile of raked leaves, pops back up without missing a beat, and keeps sprinting.

MADELINE (breathless, running)

KOBE! STAY!

Kobe stops. Looks back. Waits exactly two seconds.

KOBE

(bolts again)

VEDA (laughing, running)

He waited two seconds!! That's twice as long as he usually waits!

OLIVIA skids to a stop at a fork in the road, rocks flying up as her sneakers scrape to a halt, scanning the ground.

OLIVIA

He went left! There's bandage fluff everywhere!

They round the corner just in time to see Kobe's legs disappear behind the old toolshed at the edge of the park — and to hear him howling as he gives chase.

CUT TO:
SCENE 4 — EXT. BEHIND THE TOOLSHED — NIGHT 7:00–9:00

The team catches up, panting, to find Kobe sitting proudly on top of a fallen scarecrow, tail going full speed, long tongue dangling out of his mouth, a scrap of bandage looped around the scarecrow's arm.

MADELINE

Kobe. Little buddy. What did you do?!

OLIVIA (examining the scene)

Wait — look. The scarecrow's arm is bent all the way back. And there's rope, snapped clean through.

VEDA

I knew it. The scarecrow attacked the mummy!

MADELINE

Scarecrows can't move, Veda.

VEDA

What makes you a scarecrow expert?

Olivia picks up the chewed rope, studying it closely.

OLIVIA

This didn't snap. It was eaten. Look how frayed it is at the top, not the bottom.

CUT TO:
SCENE 5 — THE REVEAL — EXT. TOWN SQUARE — LATER 9:00–11:30

Olivia lays it out for the team, gesturing between the mummy statue and the scarecrow.

OLIVIA

Here's what happened. Some animal must have chewed through the rope — could've been a werewolf, or maybe a vampire bat. When the rope broke, the scarecrow swung into the mummy statue, and its arm caught the wrapping and pulled the whole thing loose as it fell.

MADELINE

And it dragged along the ground all the way behind the toolshed...

OLIVIA

...where Kobe found it and decided it was the best toy in the world.

Kobe, still wearing a scrap of bandage like a scarf, barks proudly.

VEDA

Wait. Werewolves and vampire bats don't live downtown. Who else could have chewed the rope?

Olivia slowly turns toward Kobe — covered head to tail in rope and straw.

OLIVIA

No, no — not a werewolf, not a vampire bat. Just a silly puppy who loves to snack on everything.

ALL (at the same time)

KOBEEE!!

MADELINE (smiling, shaking her head)

Mystery solved. Case closed.

CUT TO:
SCENE 6 — WRAP-UP — EXT. TOWN SQUARE — NIGHT 11:30–14:00

The team rewraps the mummy statue together, working as one, while Kobe "helps" mostly by getting tangled in the bandages himself. Veda tries to grab the loose end to help him — and gets wrapped right along with him. When it stops, the two of them are stuck together as a single mummy, wrapped head to toe — only Veda's eyes and lips visible, and Kobe's eyes and ears poking out through the bandages beside her.

VEDA (muffled, only her eyes and lips showing)

These two crime-solving mummies just solved the case. On the next case, we could use a little help?

Madeline and Olivia look at the two of them, unimpressed, hands on hips. Without a word, they each grab a loose end of bandage and yank — hard. Veda and Kobe spin in place like a top, bandages unraveling off both of them and flying loose into the air.

MADELINE (laughing)

Good work, team. Olivia, great catch on the rope.

OLIVIA

Thanks. Veda, your scarecrow theory was... creative.

VEDA (finally free, proudly)

That's what leaders do!! We solve cases. Hopefully next time you and Madeline will pitch in.

Madeline gives a huge eye roll while she crouches down to scratch Kobe's ears. His leg instantly starts kicking.

MADELINE

And you. Best nose on the team. The absolute worst listener on the team.

Kobe gives her face one giant, slobbery lick — ear to chin, drool and all. Madeline wipes her cheek, laughing. Then Kobe's ears perk up: he spots a shadowy figure, across the square.

With the bandages still wrapped around him, Kobe takes off in a full sprint after the shadowy figure. Mid-yard, he skids to a stop, plants his paws, and points his nose straight up at the full moon.

KOBE

(one long, dramatic howl — then bolts, instantly)

The leftover strip of mummy bandage, still looped around his middle, streams out behind him and flaps wildly in the wind as he sprints off.

ALL THREE (in unison)

KOBE!

MADELINE

Looks like we've got another case.

OLIVIA (pulling out her magnifying glass)

I'm on it!

VEDA (bored look, eyes rolled)

I already solved it!

They take off running after Kobe, laughing, bandage still fluttering, as the mummy statue stands finally rewrapped behind them, the full moon glowing above it.
